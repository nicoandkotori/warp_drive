Original FBX supplied by the user from the authorized Sketchfab download.
Changes: assigned blue, blue-white, warm-white, gold and orange-red emission
materials with varied brightness; added cameras, black world and gentle glow.
Star positions and geometry are preserved. No new stars or nebulae added.
This is an artistic galaxy, not a scientifically accurate Milky Way map.
Original import contains 6001 mesh objects. Approximate center spread:
X: -20.84..20.93; Y: -20.86..20.74; Z: -0.73..0.73.

Open galaxy_6000_colored.blend in Blender. Middle-mouse drag orbits,
mouse wheel zooms, Shift+middle-mouse pans. Numpad 0 enters the camera.
Use Z > Rendered to see emissive materials against black space.

Reference-based nebula revision:
File: galaxy_reference_nebula.blend; builder: reference_nebula.py.
Added a real procedural volume: broad fractured blue-grey arms, warm central
emission, multiscale dust absorption and sparse rose emission regions.
All 6000 stars retained. Hidden etoile.6001, the oversized source prototype
at the origin, replacing its visible solid ball with a volumetric nucleus.
This additional volume is our procedural modification, not part of the
original Sketchfab download or the proprietary SpiralForge product.
